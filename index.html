Case studies

How I do it, in detail.

Trestique
Beauty · E-commerce · USA

$812K in YTD sales and $309K net profit at a 36% margin.

Problem

Solid creatives but no structure: rising CPP, expensive acquisition, and blended ROAS hiding the real picture.

Solution

Systematic UGC creator program testing new faces monthly, Advantage+ cold traffic fully separated from warm retargeting, and Meta spend scaled while holding a 36% net margin.

Result

$812,361 in sales (+161% YoY), $309,534 net profit, 36% margin, tracked in TripleWhale, not vanity pixel numbers. Last 30 days alone: $161K in sales.

Infamous Paintball
Sporting Goods · E-commerce · USA

1,116 purchases at $8.79 CPP and 23x ROAS in 30 days.

Problem

High-AOV catalog ($100–$500+) with campaigns competing against each other for the same audiences, muddying the algorithm's optimization signals.

Solution

Product-split structure: one campaign per category (harnesses, goggles, bags, apparel…), each with its own budget and creative testing environment, plus a full-funnel retargeting layer on top.

Result

$229K attributed revenue from $9.8K spend in 30 days. Six months later: 54 campaigns, 770 ads, remarketing holding 43x ROAS and the best creative at 148x.

23x avg ROAS$8.79 CPP43x remarketing148x best creative
Effing Gear
Workwear · E-commerce · USA

Inherited 322 chaotic campaigns. Rebuilt to 1,083 purchases at $26 CPP.

Problem

Account fragmented across 322 campaigns left by previous media buyers: budget sitting in dormant campaigns, no testing system, no evergreen structure.

Solution

Consolidated everything into two clean layers: evergreen scaling for proven winners and a dedicated testing layer that validates every creative before it gets budget. UGC hooks tied to blue-collar identity.

Result

1,083 purchases at $26.19 CPP and $47.3K revenue in 30 days. Best single campaign: 471 purchases at $22.71 CPP.
